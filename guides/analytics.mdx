## Performance KPIs

At the top of the Analytics dashboard, six high-level metrics give you an immediate snapshot of your platform's performance based on your selected date range:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Total Calls" icon="phone">
    The absolute volume of inbound and outbound calls processed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Total Minutes" icon="stopwatch">
    The aggregate number of voice minutes consumed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Success Rate" icon="bullseye">
    The percentage of calls where the agent successfully completed its objective.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Avg Duration" icon="clock">
    The average length of your real-time conversations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Active Agents" icon="users-robot">
    The number of unique AI agents that fielded calls during the period.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sentiment" icon="face-smile">
    The overall average sentiment (positive/neutral/negative) of your callers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Visualizations & Trends

Agni provides a powerful suite of interactive charts to help you visualize data trends and optimize your agents' performance.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Usage Trend" icon="chart-line">
    Tracks calls and minutes over time. Because Agni operates on a usage-based pricing model, this chart directly correlates to your platform spend and growth.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Call Outcomes" icon="chart-pie">
    A comparative breakdown plotting **Successful** versus **Failed** calls, allowing you to quickly spot routing issues or prompt inefficiencies over time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sentiment Analysis" icon="face-meh">
    An AI-powered breakdown of caller sentiment based on conversational analytics. *(Note: This feature is rolling out soon).*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agent Performance" icon="ranking-star">
    Compares success rates across different agent deployments. Use this to A/B test system prompts, voices, or tool configurations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Call Duration" icon="wave-square">
    Plots your average call duration in seconds over time, helping you measure agent efficiency and conversation length.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Peak Hours" icon="chart-area">
    Visualizes call volume by the hour of the day, split between **Business hours** and **Off hours** to help you understand concurrency demands.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Filtering and Exporting Data

In the top right corner of the dashboard, you can control the scope of your analytics to generate custom reports:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter by Date" icon="calendar-days">
    Use the quick-select pills (**7 Days**, **30 Days**, **90 Days**) or the date picker to instantly recalculate your dashboard metrics for a specific timeframe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export to CSV" icon="download">
    Click the **Export** button to download your current analytics view. You can easily ingest this data into your CRM, billing software, or internal reporting tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>
