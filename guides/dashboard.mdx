Once you log in, you are greeted by the Agni Dashboard. This is your central command center for monitoring agent performance, tracking usage, visualizing live traffic, and navigating your voice AI infrastructure.

## At-a-Glance Metrics

At the top of your dashboard, you can quickly assess the high-level health and performance of your voice agents in real time:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Total Minutes" icon="clock">
    The aggregate number of minutes consumed by all your agents. Check the billing page for your current costs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Active Agents" icon="users-robot">
    The total number of agents currently deployed and active in your workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live Calls" icon="phone-volume">
    The number of concurrent calls happening right now, marked with a LIVE indicator.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cost" icon="money-bill-wave">
    Your total accrued usage cost based on your plan's per-minute rate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Success Rate" icon="bullseye">
    The overall percentage of calls where agents reached a successful objective.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Conversion" icon="chart-line-up">
    Your overall conversion rate for targeted outcomes (e.g., appointments booked, leads qualified).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

---

## Live Visualizations & Analytics

The center of the dashboard provides a suite of interactive widgets to help you monitor system load and campaign outcomes dynamically.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Global Reach" icon="globe">
    A live 3D visualization showing exactly where in the world your active calls are currently taking place. Useful for understanding your geographic distribution.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Usage Trends" icon="chart-line">
    Track your minute consumption and total call volume over a rolling 30-day period. Toggle between **Day**, **Week**, or **Month** views to spot patterns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Call Outcomes" icon="chart-pie">
    A trailing 14-day breakdown plotting **Successful** versus **Failed** calls. A declining success rate usually signals prompt or routing issues worth investigating.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Today's Activity" icon="chart-area">
    An hourly breakdown comparing your **Inbound** versus **Outbound** traffic for the current day. Use this to understand peak demand windows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Active Agents" icon="robot">
    A quick view of which agents are currently handling live calls, with a shortcut to immediately create a new agent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Top Regions" icon="map-location-dot">
    A demographic breakdown counting the total number of calls per country and region.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Workspace Navigation

The left sidebar is your primary map for navigating your Agni workspace. It is divided into three core sections:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Platform" icon="layer-group">
    Your daily operational views and data tracking.
    - **[Dashboard](/guides/dashboard):** Your high-level overview and metrics.
    - **[Analytics](/guides/analytics):** Deep dive into usage, latency, and call outcomes.
    - **[Calls](/guides/calls):** Review call logs, transcripts, and recordings.
    - **[Appointments](/guides/appointments):** Manage calendar events synced via Cal.com or GoHighLevel.
    - **[Inbound Calls](/guides/inbound-calls):** Configure routing for incoming traffic.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Management" icon="sliders">
    The core configuration of your voice AI agents.
    - **Campaigns:** Manage and monitor active outbound campaigns.
    - **[Agents](/guides/agents):** Create, update, and deploy your AI agents.
    - **[Tools](/guides/tools):** Build custom functions and webhooks.
    - **Contacts:** Manage your CRM and address book.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Operations" icon="briefcase">
    The backend infrastructure of your workspace.
    - **[Phone Numbers](/guides/phone-numbers):** Buy, import, and release phone numbers.
    - **[Billing](/guides/billing):** Manage your subscription and view invoices.
    - **[Settings](/guides/settings):** Configure organization, security, and integrations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
