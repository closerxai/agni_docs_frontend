## At-a-Glance Metrics

At the top of your dashboard, below your personalized monthly summary, you can quickly assess the high-level health and performance of your voice agents in real time. 

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Total Minutes" icon="clock">
    The aggregate number of minutes consumed by all your agents.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Active Agents" icon="users-robot">
    The total number of agents currently deployed and active.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live Calls" icon="phone-volume">
    The number of concurrent calls happening right now (marked with a LIVE indicator).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cost" icon="money-bill-wave">
    Your total accrued usage cost, calculated at the flat $0.07/min rate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Success Rate" icon="bullseye">
    The overall percentage of calls that reached a successful objective.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Conversion" icon="chart-line-up">
    Your overall conversion rate for targeted outcomes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Live Visualizations & Analytics

The center of the dashboard provides a suite of interactive widgets to help you monitor system load and campaign outcomes dynamically.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Global Reach" icon="globe">
    A live 3D visualization showing exactly where in the world your active calls are currently taking place.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Usage Trends" icon="chart-line">
    Track your minute consumption and total call volume over a rolling 30-day period. Toggle between **Day**, **Week**, or **Month** views.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Call Outcomes" icon="chart-pie">
    A trailing 14-day breakdown plotting your **Successful** versus **Failed** calls to help you easily spot routing or prompt issues.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Today's Activity" icon="chart-area">
    An hourly breakdown comparing your **Inbound** versus **Outbound** traffic for the current day.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Active Agents" icon="robot">
    A quick view of which agents are currently handling live calls, with a shortcut to immediately create a new agent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Top Regions" icon="map-location-dot">
    A demographic breakdown counting the total number of calls per Country and Region.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Workspace Navigation

The left sidebar is your primary map for navigating your Agni workspace. It is divided into three core sections:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Platform" icon="layer-group">
    Your daily operational views and data tracking.
    - **Dashboard:** Your high-level overview and metrics.
    - **Analytics:** Deep dive into usage, latency, and call outcomes.
    - **Calls:** Review call logs, transcripts, and recordings.
    - **Appointments:** Manage calendar events synced via Cal.com or GoHighLevel.
    - **Inbound Calls:** Configure routing for incoming traffic.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Management" icon="sliders">
    The core configuration of your voice AI brains.
    - **Campaigns:** Manage and monitor active outbound campaigns.
    - **Agents:** Create, update, and deploy your AI agents.
    - **Tools:** Build custom functions and webhooks.
    - **Contacts:** Manage your CRM and address book.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Operations" icon="briefcase">
    The backend infrastructure of your workspace.
    - **Phone Numbers:** Buy, import, and release phone numbers.
    - **Billing:** Manage your subscription and view invoices.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
